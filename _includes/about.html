Robot-Assisted Living Laboratory is a state-of-the-art robotic laboratory led byDr. Jihong Zhu.We are based in theInstitute for Safe AutonomyUniversity of York, the UK. Our work is driven by a commitment to technical excellence and a passion for developing robotic solutions to real-world challenges. We aim to pioneer the future of assistive robotics for caregiving and accelerating scientific discovery. To achieve this, we integrate multiple sensory modalities, including vision and tactile sensing, and employ advanced AI, control, and machine learning techniques.
